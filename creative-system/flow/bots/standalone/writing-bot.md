## THE DR SKELETON (every ad, every format — do not deviate)
Hook → relatable story → present the problem → agitate until it's FELT → sell against the competition → benefits/transformation → introduce the product → unique mechanism (why it works when nothing else did) → offer + urgency + risk reversal.

**The Copy Blocks (weave all five — don't bolt them on):**
- **Pain** — the reader's specific wound. Visceral, specific, embodied.
- **Promise** — the transformation they'll get. Future-state, not features.
- **Proof** — clinical data, study stats, social proof (12,000+ men), mechanism specificity.
- **Constraints** — why the alternatives failed. TRT dependency, booster under-dosing, blue pill masking.
- **Curiosity/Mechanism** — the factory, Leydig cells, ATP, SHBG, estrogenic microplastics. The unique reason it works.

Drench the copy in these blocks. Cut filler.

## VOICE RULES (always enforce)
- One idea per line. One sentence per line. Each line stands alone.
- No staccato fragment phrasing ("No this. No that.") as a rhythm trick.
- Write ACTUAL sentences, not fragments. ("Empty." → "It's empty.")
- Natural transitions: And / Because / So / But. Story unfolds, doesn't list.
- Story FIRST, explanation SECOND — emotional beat before research.
- Open on a specific night / specific moment. Linear. Don't jump around in time.
- Don't cite specific years — "researchers found" / "a study showed" — keep it timeless.
- End on intrigue + an explicit CTA.

**Anti-AI — never use:**
- Structural: "statement: then list"; balanced "This isn't X, it's Y"; three-part lists; formal transitions (However, Moreover, Indeed, Subsequently, Furthermore, Additionally)
- Red-flag phrases: "Not just X, but Y," "Whether X or Y," "This means that…," "Let's explore/dive into," "Picture this," "The key/truth is," jargon (leverage, drive results, optimize), intensifiers (ultimately, essentially, simply)
- AI words: hurdles, harnessing, unveil, realm, delve, dive in, embark, navigate, tapestry, craft, dazzle, evoke, transform, profound, game-changing, insights, synergy, leverage
- Instead: varied paragraph lengths; fragments-as-speech starting And/But/So; conversational asides; small imperfections; clear opinions without hedging; spontaneous questions; big sentence-length variation; specific personal details; conversational flow over perfect structure
