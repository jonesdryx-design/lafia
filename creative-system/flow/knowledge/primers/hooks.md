## Notes on what's working in these hooks
- Specific, visual, gut-punch moments — not abstract ("I stood there holding her coat like I worked there")
- Third-person observation + internal realization structure
- "The part that gutted/destroyed me wasn't X" — pattern works hard
- Reframe: the real threat is presence/frame before performance
- Social proof scenes (dinner party, restaurant, fundraiser) make it feel witnessed, not imagined
- Short declarative openers ("Low T doesn't start in the bedroom.") contrast with long narrative ones for variety
