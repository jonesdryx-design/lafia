## Notes
- The best ones pair the frame concept with a specific scene ("Dinner Table" beats "in daily life")

- H17 (microplastics) is a mechanism headline — good for the segment who's heard about environmental T disruption
- H16 (12,000+ men) is social proof — different from all others, use when social proof angle is needed
- Avoid "Your T is low" framing — the angle is frame/presence loss, not a lab number
