## POSITIONING

### The Positioning Formula
```
WHO YOU HELP + WHO YOU ARE + WHAT OUTCOME + HOW YOU DO IT + WHY NOW
```
Don't need all five, but each adds power. Clarity beats cleverness.

### Positioning Equation (full)
```
POSITIONING = (Formula + Category + Alchemy + AntiPosition) × (Proof × Specificity)
```

**Category Ownership (-EST Framework):**
Own a category where you're #1. If you can't be #1, create a new category.
- MOST, FASTEST, ONLY, FIRST, YOUNGEST/OLDEST
- Must be objective and measurable. "Best" means nothing, "MOST" means everything.

**Live examples members invented:**
- "1st copywriter in my family"
- "The Princess of Print"
- "I write leads that beat the control 9 out of 10 times" (backed by a simple spreadsheet — date of test, product category, result)

**Alchemy Factor:** Transform perceived weaknesses into strengths
- "I'm expensive and slow" → "Because I actually test everything"
- "I'm new" → "I'm hungry and will outwork anyone"
- The Avis Effect: "We're #2, so we try harder"
- Overseas/international → "You wake up to finished work"

**AntiPosition:** What you DON'T do is as powerful as what you DO
- Creates clarity through contrast
- "I don't do meetings, revisions, or work with beginners"
- Position against something — a shared enemy, a method, a market

**Brian Speronello's rule:** Don't say "you're leaving money on the table" to a 9-figure business owner — that's not the flex you think it is. Instead: *"Hey I noticed you're doing X — how is that working for you? Because I've always done Y and it's produced Z."*

**"If you try to get everyone, you get nobody."**

## PROOF BUILDING

### Proof Pyramid
```
Belief → Anecdote → Pattern → System → Reputation → Authority
```
- Level 0: "I believe I can" ($0)
- Level 1: "I did it once" ($1–2K projects)
- Level 2: "Multiple times" ($5K projects)
- Level 3: "Consistently" ($10K+ projects)
- Level 4: "Known for this" ($25K+ projects)
- Level 5: "Wrote the book" ($50K+ projects)

**Proof as Currency:** One screenshot beats 1000 words of copy. Skill without proof = worthless.

### Demonstration Hierarchy
```
Talk < Show < Prove < Guarantee < Share Risk
```
Each level is 10x more powerful than the previous.

### How to Build Proof Fast
- Post wins in your community — business owners inside groups hire directly from posts (Bashir Musa case study: posted wins inside Genesis → business owners hired him)
- Share wins without naming the client: "Talk about the work and results, not the company name" — NDA-safe
- Build a track record doc: date, product category, win/loss. Small but specific beats vague and large.
- Work testimonials into contracts: offer to knock $1K off the fee in exchange for a video testimonial interview
- Offer to do the first project for the result/testimonial (Rookie stage — ethical and expected)
- Barter for proof: "A girl asked Krista Mashore to post her business in exchange for free botox — Krista said yes."
- Cosmin case (17, no proof, no reviews): made a mini VSL instead of text proposals → landed Jason Fladlien → $500K+ lifetime

## OUTREACH

### Volume Math (Luke's numbers)
- 1% Response Rate
- 25% Close Rate
- 20 Follow-Ups Before Response (on average)
- Translation: to get 1 client you need ~400 messages — but to get 20 follow-ups per prospect, you need 8,000 total messages for consistent output
- **"Whatever number you tell us you're doing, we automatically take half." — Mario**
- "Unless you do 20 follow-ups with 150 people, don't complain."

### The 7 Core Outbound Tactics
1. Cold Email
2. Cold DM
3. Platform Application (Upwork, Hiry, Marketer Hire)
4. Value-First Outreach
5. Warm Introduction / Referral
6. Physical Mail (Lumpy Mail)
7. Multi-Touch Sequence (7+ touches, 45 days)

### Cold Email

**Subject lines that work:**
- "Quick question" (Andrew Purdum's all-time favorite)
- "Thought you should know about this" (Scott Hartley)
- "Did you see this?" / "Noticed something recently..." (Bradley Tanenbaum)

**Body approach:**
- Lead with a specific observation about them, then your proof
- "I've spotted X ways to improve your website. Cool if I send them over?" (open loop / pattern interrupt)
- Follow up with value: "I got success with your X" cuts through noise
- Don't make the mistake of telling big companies they're "leaving money on the table" — condescending

**The AWE Framework (Brian Speronello / Szilard):**
- **A**cknowledge their current work
- **W**edge and alternative
- **E**laborate on it

**The CHARM Framework (Jon Buchan):**
- **C**onfession: Radical honesty that disarms
- **H**umanity: Deliberate imperfection
- **A**bsurd Anchor: Memorable pattern interrupt
- **R**eciprocity: Value first, always
- **M**enu: Multiple response options

**5-Part Message Formula:**
```
HOOK × CONTEXT × VALUE × PROOF × CTA
```
- Hook: Get attention (not sell)
- Context: Why reaching out NOW
- Value: Specific promise
- Proof: Why believe you
- CTA: Clear next step

**7-Touch Minimum:** Touch 1–7 over 45 days. Different angle each time. Add value each touch. Use different channels.

## OFFER CONSTRUCTION

### Four-Tier Offer Structure
- **Foot-in-the-Door:** Zero friction, often free, demonstrates value
- **Entry Offer:** Small paid project to build momentum
- **Expansion Offer:** Core money-maker
- **Premium Offer:** High-ticket anchor

**Always Be Proposing (Brian Speronello):**
Instead of Always Be Closing → Always Be Proposing. Always propose new specific ways you can help your clients. Then charge for the added value.

### 8-Point HOT Entry Checklist
1. Instant Value (results visible immediately)
2. Zero Friction (no complex setup)
3. Low Risk (under $3K)
4. Clear Need (obvious pain point)
5. Low Time (<1 week delivery)
6. High Perception (looks like 10x value)
7. Repeatable (same process for multiple clients)
8. Natural Upsell (leads to bigger project)

### Offer Heat Model
```
Heat = PAIN × NOVELTY × TIMING × PROOF × MOMENTUM
```
Score each 1–10. Product over 10,000 = hot offer.

### Offer Framing
- "FB ad audit" = meh
- "I will find the leaks in your FB ad funnel" = strong
- Audits work better when you already have authority
- Frame around outcomes, not deliverables
- Business owners don't care what you do — they care what they get

**The Agency Ladder:**
```
Task Level (1X income) → Function Level (10X) → Outcome Level (100X) → Growth Level (1000X)
```
"More money requires agency, not more tasks." — Amy Gravlee

## PRICING TACTICS

- Business owners look at results, not costs. "I'd pay $10 to make $12." — Carolyn Santo
- Price based on potential value to them, not on what you think you're worth
- Charge higher by framing around outcomes, not deliverables
- Stop worrying about PayPal/Stripe fees — removing payment friction makes clients more likely to pay AND more likely to rehire
- Send a Stripe link — remove friction from the transaction
- Price scarcity: be transparent about WHY supply is limited. Authentic scarcity works.
- Never go backward on price (The Price Paradox: charge nothing to get proof → use proof to charge everything)

**The Ratchet Framework:**
- Standards only go up, never down
- Every Sunday: fire worst client or raise their rate
- Increase lowest rate 10%
- Creates irreversible progress

**Runway = Power:** 6 months saved = can walk away from anything. 2 weeks runway = desperation. Negotiation is determined by who can leave.

**Performance vs. Base:**
- At $1M+/month brands: 2.5–5% of a scaling ad = thousands/month from one creative
- Base + upside is always worth structuring
