# Segment Builder — The WHAT × WHO × WHY Framework
Source: Exodus App (segment-builder tool) + Creative Diversity Decoded

A segment is a named persona built from three dimensions intersecting:
- **WHAT** (outcomes / columns) — what result they want or problem they have
- **WHO** (demographics / rows) — who they are in the world
- **WHY** (facets, stacked above) — what's true for them right now that shapes the buy

Nothing selected = **TAM** (Total Addressable Market). Cut it down by picking combinations.

---

## THE THREE DIMENSIONS

### WHAT — Outcomes (Columns)
The specific result the person wants, or the specific problem they have.
Each outcome can have sub-slices (more specific versions of the same outcome).

*Flow examples:*
- Restore marriage presence
- Restart physical drive
- Recover decision-making certainty
- Improve morning energy
- Reverse the slow fade

Sub-slices: "Restore marriage presence" → [wife stopped leaning on me] [she's drifting emotionally] [we feel like roommates]

### WHO — Demographics (Rows)
The factual framework of who this person is.

*Flow examples:*
- Married men 40–55
- Married men 35–45 (younger cohort, earlier problem recognition)
- Divorced men 45–60 (post-marriage, looking back at what happened)
- Men in long-term relationships, not married
- Men who know they have low T (product-aware)
- Men who don't know T is the issue (unaware)

### WHY — Facets (Stacked above, 2 families create a pocket)
What's true for them RIGHT NOW that shapes why they'd buy.

The 5 facet families:

| Facet | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Belief** | What they blame for their problem |
| **Identity** | The tribe they buy as / how they see themselves |
| **State** | What's true now / what they've already tried |
| **Value** | What matters most in the buying decision |
| **Occasion** | Why now / what triggered the search |

---

## HOW TO BUILD A SEGMENT

1. **Pick an outcome** (the WHAT column) — e.g., "Restore marriage presence"
2. **Pick a sub-slice** if needed — e.g., "Wife feels emotionally distant"
3. **Pick a demographic** (the WHO row) — e.g., "Married men 40–55"
4. **Stack facets** (the WHY) — pick 2 facets to create a specific pocket persona

The intersection of WHAT + WHO + WHY = a named persona.

### Segment 1: The Invisible Man
- **WHAT:** Restore presence / frame in marriage
- **WHO:** Married men 42–55, professional, household income $100K+
- **WHY facets:**
  - Belief: "This is just what getting older looks like"
  - State: "Wife is managing things I used to lead"

### Segment 2: The Skeptic Who's Tried Everything
- **WHAT:** Restart physical drive + energy
- **WHO:** Married men 38–52, has tried supplements before (TRT curious but hesitant)
- **WHY facets:**
  - State: "Already tried testosterone boosters / TRT consultation"
  - Belief: "Supplements don't work for me"
- **Awareness level:** Solution-aware (knows options exist, has been burned)
- **Lead angle:** Failed Alternatives — "Here's why what you tried didn't work"
- **Hook register:** Failed-solution comparison, mechanism explanation

### Segment 3: The Man Who Doesn't Know Why
- **WHAT:** Recover the man I used to be (identity)
- **WHO:** Married men 38–50, hasn't connected symptoms to T yet
- **WHY facets:**
  - Belief: "It's stress / work / getting older"
  - State: "Bloodwork came back normal"
- **Awareness level:** Unaware-to-problem-aware edge
- **Lead angle:** Paradox — "Why does your blood panel say normal but your marriage feel wrong?"
- **Hook register:** Symptom list (Notes app format), editorial/news format

### Segment 4: The TRT Hesitant
- **WHAT:** Natural alternative to TRT
- **WHO:** Men 40–60 who've been told TRT is the answer, are hesitant about dependency/needles
- **WHY facets:**
  - Value: "No injections, no dependency, natural approach"
  - State: "Was prescribed TRT but didn't fill it"
- **Awareness level:** Solution-aware / product-aware
- **Lead angle:** Comparison — "TRT vs natural factory restart: what they don't tell you"
- **Hook register:** Comparison chart, mechanism explanation, cost-of-TRT math

### Segment 5: The Single Man (Gap Segment — not yet built)

## THE EXODUS SEGMENT BUILDER INTERFACE
*(Reference for using the tool)*

**Navigation tabs:** Runs · Library · Ideas · Memes · Segments · Settings

**Segment map formula:**
WHAT (outcomes ↓ + sub-slices) × WHO (demographics →) × WHY (facets, stacked above) → named persona. Nothing selected = TAM.

**Build process:**
1. Set product word + brand name in setup fields
2. Add outcome columns (the WHAT) then click "+ sub" to add sub-slices
3. Add demographic rows (the WHO) with "+ add chips"
4. Add facet pills from the 5 families (Belief / Identity / State / Value / Occasion)
5. Click outcome + demographic (or just a cell) to generate persona
6. "Save persona" adds it to the queue of segments to target

**Pre-built examples in the tool:** Flow · Lumen · PestFree
