# Angles — The 55 Psychological Frames of Persuasion
Source: Creative Diversity Decoded (Notion)

**Core Principle:** Angles are the psychological entry points through which information becomes
persuasive by aligning with specific mental states and cognitive biases. They transform raw
facts into compelling narratives that hijack attention and create urgency.

We don't change minds with new information. We change HOW people attend to information they
already have. The angle shapes attention → perception → belief → action.

**The Multiplication Effect:** Take any single fact and multiply it by different angles to create
diverse entry points into the same core idea. Same information, completely different
psychological experience.

## CATEGORY A — DISCOVERY & REVELATION FRAMES
*The mind's attraction to new information and pattern completion*

**1. New Discovery**
Fresh breakthrough that didn't exist before.
> "Scientists just discovered why some people never get Alzheimer's"
> "Brand new Harvard study reveals the real cause of belly fat"
Flow: "Researchers just identified the specific pathway low T uses to kill a man's presence before his drive"

**2. Accidental Discovery**
Serendipitous finding while looking for something else.
> "Doctor accidentally cures arthritis while treating insomnia"
> "Mom stumbles upon autism treatment while baking bread"
Flow: "I was researching ATP for athletic performance. I stumbled on something about testosterone nobody talks about."

**3. Lost Wisdom**
Ancient knowledge rediscovered and validated.
> "The 400-year-old Japanese secret to living past 100"
> "Ancient Roman cure for baldness rediscovered in Vatican archives"
Flow: "The compound Andean farmers used for generations — researchers just confirmed why it works"

**4. Missing Piece**
The overlooked element that changes everything.
> "The one mineral missing from every diet (that controls everything)"
> "The hidden factor in every autoimmune disease"
Flow: "Your testosterone protocol is missing one thing. And it's not a hormone."

**5. Root Cause**
The real problem behind the surface symptoms.
> "It's not your thyroid — here's what's really making you exhausted"
> "The real reason you can't save money (hint: not your spending)"
Flow: "It's not low T. It's what's suppressing your factory's output."

**6. Translation / Decoder**
Making complex information suddenly clear.
> "What your doctor really means when they say 'normal' test results"
> "Finally: someone explains crypto in plain English"
Flow: "What 'normal range' on your testosterone panel actually means (and why it's destroying your marriage)"

**7. Paradox / Contradiction**
Counterintuitive truth that defies common sense.
> "Why eating more fat makes you skinny"
> "How doing nothing cured my chronic fatigue"
Flow: "Why fixing your T has nothing to do with your T level"

**8. Unintended Consequences**
Unexpected positive side effects.
> "Blood pressure medication's bizarre side effect: photographic memory"
> "How fixing my gut accidentally cured my depression"
Flow: "I took this to fix my energy. My wife noticed something else first."
