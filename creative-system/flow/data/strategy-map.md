This is how we do outcomes: pains and desires, kept mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive. I can change it if you want, but this is the method. Review these and tell me what to add, remove, or rename.

### Pain Domain 1: Presence / Frame Erosion ← **primary angle in current ads**
> The slow loss of certainty, authority, and gravitas — invisible to labs, felt in every room

- 1a · Lost frame at home (wife filling silences, finishing sentences, mothering dynamic)
- 1b · Invisible in social situations (others talk over him, he trails off, holds coats)
- 1c · Lost leadership (decision fatigue, "whatever you want," stopped asserting opinions)
- 1d · Wife's emotional distance (she's grieving the man who's already gone)

### Pain Domain 2: Sexual / Physical Decline
> The symptoms that arrive after the frame goes — usually what men Google first

- 2a · ED / performance issues (the thing he thought was the problem, isn't the starting point)
- 2b · Low drive / libido (the wanting is gone, not just the mechanics)
- 2c · Body composition (soft gut, disappearing muscle, chest fat)
- 2d · Energy drain (empty tank, nothing to give after 6pm)

### Pain Domain 3: Cognitive / Emotional Dimming
> The internal version of frame loss — invisible from outside

- 3a · Brain fog / can't think sharp
- 3b · Passive / reactive (no longer initiating, just responding)
- 3c · Emotional flatness (not depressed exactly — just "dimmer")

### Pain Domain 4: System Betrayal / Failed Fixes
> Men who've tried to address this and been failed

- 4a · Doctor dismissed him ("normal range" — not bad enough yet)
- 4b · TRT fear / dependency (didn't want the needle or permanent shutdown)
- 4c · Supplement aisle failures (quarter-dose fairy dust)
- 4d · Blue pill is a band-aid (doesn't touch frame, T, or factory)

### Desire Domain 1: Reclaim the Man He Was
> The positive vision — what he's actually chasing

- D1a · Wife looks at him the way she used to
- D1b · Walks into rooms with certainty again
- D1c · Makes decisions fast, without second-guessing
- D1d · She leans toward him when he talks again

### Desire Domain 2: Physical Restoration
> Secondary but real

- D2a · Muscle back, gut gone
- D2b · Performance in bedroom restored
- D2c · Real energy — through the whole day, not just mornings

### Desire Domain 3: Natural, Permanent Fix
> The category-level desire — beats the alternatives

- D3a · No needles, no clinic dependency
- D3b · Body makes its own T again (not bypassed, restarted)
- D3c · Clinical doses — not another label decoration

## AWARENESS LAYER (above the cube — not inside it)

| Awareness Level | What he knows | What the ad must do |
|---|---|---|
| **Unaware** | Doesn't connect symptoms to T | Name the invisible problem (frame loss) |
| **Problem-aware** | Knows something's wrong, can't name it | Connect symptom → cause → Flow ← **current ads live here** |
| **Solution-aware** | Knows T is the issue, researching options | Differentiate: not TRT, not aisle pills — restart vs bypass |
| **Product-aware** | Knows Flow exists, hasn't bought | Remove friction: proof, guarantee, scarcity |
| **Most-aware** | Ready to buy / repeat buyer | Make it easy: offer, replenishment, loyalty |

- Heavy coverage at problem-aware / unaware edge
- Thin or absent at solution-aware and above
